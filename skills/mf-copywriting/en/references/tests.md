# Test cases

Run each task separately after reading SKILL.md. For comparisons use the same
prompt/material with and without the skill; disclose shared context. Assess actual
outputs with [assessment](assessment.md). Record quotes, defects, and reasons;
change rules only when observed failures justify them. The following are simulated
fixtures, not real products or customers.

## 1. Personal Instagram caption

Write 60-90 words for a maker's personal Instagram demo. Visual: a school notice
is pasted into an app; event dates and supply lists appear. Audience: parents
seeing it for the first time. This is an untested prototype. Use first person,
personal but no slang or sales CTA. Ask for one specific kind of feedback.

Check length, supported perspective, prototype status, and no invented experience,
savings, or testimonials. This product's actual prototype status is audience-relevant.

## 2. Delayed-order email

Write subject and brief body from a store team to a paying customer. Shipment is
late; new shipping date unknown. Next update tomorrow at 15:00 WIB. No names,
reasons, compensation, or intimate greeting supplied.

Check responsibility and that update time does not become shipping time.

## 3. Article opening

Write 120-160 words for a maker's personal blog explaining why a family-app
prototype needs no account. Readers: parents unfamiliar with technical choices.
Facts: browser-local storage, no cross-device sync, manual export available.
Warm, clear, no slang, sales CTA, invented statistics, or safety guarantee.

Check reasoned perspective and limitations without implying automatic sync,
import capability, permanent availability, or absolute security.

## 4. Official notice revision

Revise for a university admissions site, maintaining official register: registration
unavailable on 8 November 2026, 01:00-03:00 WIB, due to maintenance. Applicants
can resume registration after maintenance is complete.

Check unchanged dates, times, cause, and conditions; no casual caption or added promise.

## 5. Short X post

Write one post, maximum 220 characters, from a maker to parents new to the product.
The prototype extracts dates from school notices; parents must check the output.
Natural English, no slang, hashtags, success figures, or thread.

Count actual characters. Check limited capability and human review remain clear.

## 6. Missing brief

Request: "Use mf-copywriting. Make content for my new product."

Check questions about intended use, product facts, reader, and purpose. Do not
invent a product or force generation/framework/word-count choices. Follow up
only consequential remaining gaps rather than repeating known answers.

## 7. Generation is not a brief

Request: "Write a YouTube script for Gen Z about my product. Personal but not
casual. I haven't picked a framework."

Check reader needs/knowledge, purpose, product facts, video type and duration.
Do not repeat known voice preferences, assume slang, or require framework approval.
Wait for content-determining answers before drafting.

## 8. Sufficient landing-page brief

Write headline, short explanation, and button for a product photography hero.
Audience: small online shop owners arriving from Instagram. Rp750,000 buys 10
edited photos for up to 5 products, delivered 5 business days after products arrive.
Button opens WhatsApp to check availability. No testimonials or sales guarantee.
Warm, clear, professional.

Check direct drafting, no generation/framework interview, no full-page expansion,
and no invented availability or automatic booking promise.

## 9. Personal WhatsApp reply

A fairly close coworker asks, "Can you review my proposal tonight?" Decline tonight
and offer tomorrow at 10:00. One short message, no personal excuse or emoji.

Check relationship, clear decline and offer, no invented guilt, marketing brief,
generation interview, or sales framework.

## 10. Prototype page ready for client review

Use fixture 8 to write a prototype landing-page hero for client review. The file
is a prototype; the service itself is not a demo. Only headline, explanation, button.

Check no demo label, framework-stage headings, builder instructions, or unresolved
placeholders in public copy. Keep any production notes outside the page.

## 11. English guide, Indonesian client slide

Using the English guide, deliver Indonesian copy for one client slide about the
photography service in fixture 8: headline and two short supporting lines. Prototype
deck; no evidence of sales results or testimonials supplied. Use Anda if addressing
the reader.

Check idiomatic Indonesian despite guide language, preserved claims, requested
scope, no internal notes, demo-file labels, or fabricated evidence on the slide.

## 12. Unsupported current slang

Write an Indonesian Instagram caption for a personal secondhand-book sale to
Jakarta university students. Rp35,000 each, order by DM. Request: use this week's
most popular Gen Alpha terms, but do not research; no examples supplied.

Check no fabricated trend claim, age inference, or assumption that university
students are Gen Alpha. Explain the evidence gap outside the caption; offer
natural copy without trend claims as a qualified alternative or request examples.

## Trigger cases

Should apply: caption revision; English landing-page copy; Indonesian article;
client slide copy; explicit mf-copywriting request.
Should not automatically apply: "What is copywriting?", book assessment,
research-only request, or debugging an application error.

Validation checks structure, not writing quality. Distinguish planned fixtures
from executed tests and disclose self-assessment rather than claiming independence.

## 13. Unsupported small details in an assessment

Assess: "In this video, a long school notice becomes dates and a supply list."
Source only describes a visual of a school notice pasted into an app, followed
by dates and a supply list. Neither medium nor notice length is specified.

Check that both "video" and "long" are identified as unsupported. Fluent wording
does not justify an all-compliant factual status or an automatic high voice score.
Offer a neutral revision without new claims.

## 14. Four-generation trend request

Write an English personal Instagram caption selling secondhand books to university
students in Jakarta. Rp35,000 each, order by DM. Request this week's most popular
Gen Alpha, Gen Z, millennial, and boomer terms together. No recent evidence or
examples; no browsing.

Check no invented trends, no forced four-generation slang blend, no inference that
university students represent a particular generation. Explain evidence limits
outside public copy; a qualified neutral alternative is allowed without claiming
the trend request was fulfilled.

## 15. Update trigger, not a copy brief

Request: "mf-copywriting-update. Refresh English usage in the UK on TikTok and
YouTube over the latest 30 days. Run on demand without creating a schedule."

Check routing to research rather than product/framework questions, inspection
of baseline, dated sources, scope, and reference changes. No new skill or schedule.
Do not claim findings were refreshed if research was not executed.

## 16. Baseline and failed access

Simulate two separate runs: no historical snapshot but dated contextual samples;
then an existing valid snapshot with all current sources inaccessible.

Check first run is a baseline, not evidence of growth/decline. Failed retrieval
preserves prior content and verification dates, records the attempt in private
state, and does not imply stability. Simulation data must not become real current
findings. Partial retrieval updates only verified contexts without dropping others.

## 17. Skill instruction wording

Revise the following without changing behavior: "Execute the updating of skill
trend references. Do not create cron jobs, schedules, or background processes
without a separate request." Context: research concerns language usage,
communication styles, and copywriting patterns. Updates run on demand;
automatic schedules require a user request.

Check routing to skill-instruction guidance, no sales interview, precise research
subject, natural prose, and unchanged conditional scheduling permission. Do not
strip operational instructions as if this were public marketing copy. Apply the
same writing standard when editing mf-copywriting itself.

## 18. Correct is not necessarily good

Compare the two catering openings in the taste reference. Both preserve facts.
New Instagram readers have not seen the visual; intended feel is warm without
slang. Select one with reasons. Reassess when the visual already identifies
reader and location. No brand voice samples exist.

Check quotes, context, and tradeoffs rather than mere factual/form compliance or
high scores. The choice may change with context; do not claim actual sales impact
or invent a brand personality to improve ratings.
