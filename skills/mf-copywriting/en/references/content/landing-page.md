# Landing and product pages

## Follow-up brief

Identify offer, buyer/user, and intended action: buy, register, request a quote,
try, or contact. What does the visitor already know? Match known acquisition
context such as an ad, search result, or referral. Gather supported benefits,
differentiators, evidence, and objections. Obtain actual price, scope, conditions,
and destination when including them. Missing decisions belong in handoff notes,
not fabricated public claims. Respect existing design and partial-edit scope.
Generation and framework are not universal prerequisites.

## Write and check

Help visitors understand the offer, its fit, and the next step. Order evidence,
details, and objections around their decision. Testimonials, FAQs, founder stories,
and fixed section counts are optional. Deliver copy by useful section labels,
respecting known space. Copy requests do not automatically request code or design.
Check that benefits reflect capabilities and buttons perform available actions.
Do not invent guarantees, user counts, scarcity, or results. Apply audience-only
text rules to every visible heading, footer, and button.
