# Presentation slides

Identify audience, meeting purpose, intended decision, source material, and output:
slide copy, speaker notes, or a presentation file. Respect known slide count,
duration, and design. A presentation about a prototype differs from a prototype
of a presentation file.

Each slide advances a relevant message. Let headings state the point, with evidence
and detail suited to the audience. Avoid fixed bullet counts. Keep speaker notes
separate from visible slide copy. Production labels such as "Hook", "AIDA",
"insert image", or "demo slide" are not slide content unless they are the subject.

Statistics, client names/logos, outcomes, and testimonials require source material.
Ask for consequential missing facts or omit optional sections; record outstanding
needs outside the slides. Fictional material is allowed when an example is requested;
explain its status in handoff, retaining audience-facing identification where
necessary to avoid mistaking illustrations for real evidence.

Inspect headings, body, footers, chart labels, and buttons as audience text. Keep
production status and builder directions outside slides. Follow the applicable
presentation/design skill for file creation, layout, and visual review; this guide
controls copy rather than replacing that workflow.
