# Email

Identify sender, recipient, relationship, purpose, and email type: personal, sales,
newsletter, notice, or follow-up. For replies, use the preceding message with
identities redacted if preferred. Clarify actions, mandatory facts, deadlines,
and attachments or links that will be mentioned.

Match subject to content; supply a new subject only when needed. Match greeting
and closing to the relationship. Explain why the reader is contacted and what
comes next. Use personal details only when supplied. Newsletters follow the
reader relationship; transactional mail prioritizes clear updates, responsibility,
and available steps. Offers also use relevant [sales guidance](sales-copy.md).
Distinguish the next update from resolution time. Explain uncertainty clearly
once rather than becoming defensive. Check names, times, attachments, and promises.
Deliver a draft, not a sent email.
