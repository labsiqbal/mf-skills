# Video scripts

Identify channel, form, duration, speaker, viewers, purpose, factual material, and
available visuals. Clarify whether output needs narration, dialogue, or visual
directions. Follow up generational labels with needs and knowledge, not assumptions
about slang or short attention spans.

Write speakable sentences. Begin with relevance and develop within the duration.
Examples can clarify technical terms; let visuals carry information where helpful.
Calls to action serve the purpose rather than appearing in every section. Separate
visual directions from spoken text. Use requested structure or choose after context
is clear. Do not invent speaker experiences or copy a personality's rhythm from
one liked video. Read as speech before delivery. Label duration estimates as
estimates: pace, pauses, and visuals affect timing. Script requests do not authorize
production, animation, or rendering.
