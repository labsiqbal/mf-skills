# WhatsApp

Identify recipient, relationship, purpose, and relevant preceding messages.
Distinguish private chat, group, customer service, sales, and follow-up. Request
relevant conversation context when needed; identities may be redacted. Clarify a
consequential stance: accept, decline, request help/payment, apologize, or set a
boundary. Do not invent intimacy, feelings, excuses, or commitments.

Match address and distance to the relationship. Personal can remain polite without
email formality. Simple personal messages need no sales framework, generation
interview, subject, or formal sign-off. Emojis and abbreviations follow samples
or requests, not a blanket assumption that WhatsApp is casual. Deliver readable,
sendable text. Separate bubbles only when useful or requested. Groups/broadcasts
need context for people who missed earlier conversation. Offers also use relevant
[sales guidance](sales-copy.md). Check promises, deadlines, pressure, and reply
relevance. Drafting does not authorize sending or replying to chats.
