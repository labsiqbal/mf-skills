# Social media

Identify platform, account type, purpose, and form: caption, single post, thread,
or carousel. Read supplied visuals/descriptions and conversation context. Ask only
missing consequential questions about length, voice, and action. Do not claim to
have seen unavailable visuals.

- Instagram: add context, story, or reasons beyond what the visual shows. Deliver
  carousel text by slide when requested.
- X: focus the idea; use threads when needed. Respect the applicable/requested
  character limit and count actual final text.
- Facebook: distinguish page, group, and community; include context for newcomers.
- Other platforms: use known audience and format needs, not age or voice stereotypes.

Openings must represent the content. Hashtags, emojis, engagement questions, and
comment prompts are optional. Repeat visual information only when helpful.
For requests involving current usage or generational terms, consult
[language observations](../language-observations.md). A trend is an option when
supported by the brief, not a quota of slang to insert.
