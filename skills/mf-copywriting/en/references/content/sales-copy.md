# Sales copy

Clarify the offer, decision-maker, relevance, intended action, objections, and
available evidence. Ask about price, promotion terms, deadlines, and guarantees
only when used. Know the medium: ad, page, email, direct message, or other material.

Connect supported product benefits to known reader needs. Choose AIDA, PAS, or
direct structure as appropriate; no universal framework. Place evidence near the
claim it supports. Calls to action describe available steps. Address objections
honestly without invented threats or shame. Urgency needs real conditions.
Check consistency of benefits, price, and terms. Use the medium's guide for form
and relationship; a conversation with one prospect need not sound like a mass ad.
