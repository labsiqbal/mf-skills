# Language and voice

## Personal without forced informality

Use approved samples for vocabulary, thought patterns, and rhythm. A sample from
another writer is not permission to copy their personality or signature phrasing.
Without samples, write warmly, directly, and coherently. Match the relationship
between writer and reader. Slang, dialect, jokes, and confession need contextual
support. Show care through recognizable situations, grounded reasons, and
meaningful choices; do not invent personal stories or customer voices.

## Calibrate against selected samples

When the user selects a version, use its text as the style reference for related
work, not the model's name. Compare vocabulary, address, sentence connections,
rhythm, openings, and invitations with the rejected version. Apply observable
choices; treat unconfirmed reasons as hypotheses. Ask which passage fits when
that distinction changes direction. Reuse known preferences without another
questionnaire.

For calm, natural, polite copy, use complete sentences, familiar transitions,
and direct invitations suited to the relationship. Hooks, fragments, slang,
and contractions are choices supported by the sample, not tests of naturalness.
If the user prefers a flowing version, shortening it is not automatically an
improvement. Preserve its language choices while correcting unsupported details.
A preference is contextual; do not impose it across audiences, formats, or
languages. An Indonesian preference needs its own English calibration.

## English

Compose in idiomatic English for the intended readers rather than translating
Indonesian syntax. Prefer concrete verbs and clear references over abstract noun
chains. Use active or passive voice according to focus: "Your order has shipped"
is natural when the sender is irrelevant. Contractions suit some registers;
formal prose can remain warm without them. Follow requested spelling conventions
or established samples. Regional idioms and slang require audience evidence.

Vary sentence length with the idea, not a fixed dramatic rhythm. Explain unfamiliar
technical terms; keep recognized terms. Avoid empty promotional jargon without
turning individual words into a blacklist. Stop when the message is complete;
questions, summaries, and calls to action are purposeful choices.

## Indonesian

Compose directly in Indonesian. Choose address forms from the relationship:
"saya", "kami", and "Anda" can be personal. "Gue-lu" is not a universal naturalness
fix. Active and passive forms depend on focus; "Pesanan sudah dikirim" can be apt.
"Merupakan" is valid in definitions. Particles such as "sih", "kok", and "kan"
need a conversational reason. Adding them to stiff prose does not create voice.
Use local terminology and examples only where supported by audience context.

## Adaptation and current usage

The guide language does not determine output language. Adapt idioms to reader
culture while preserving names, facts, promises, and uncertainty. Separate requested
bilingual versions clearly; check claim equivalence rather than matching word counts.
Mix languages only if requested or supported by source voice.

For current social language or generational slang, read
[language observations](language-observations.md). General fluency is not evidence
that an expression is currently popular.

After revising, recheck meaning, certainty, promises, and length. Use complete
sentences and familiar transitions only when they help the reader follow the
thought. Naturalness does not justify extra explanation, defensive caveats, or a
change in content.

Illustrative edit: "We facilitate the management of important information" could
become "Event dates and supply lists appear in one list" only when product facts
support that specificity. Always recheck meaning after making prose concrete.

## Grounded personal voice

Illustrative material: the maker chose to show the supply list before other
details so parents can see what to prepare; no user testing yet. Generic:
"I built a useful feature for parents." More specific: "I put the supply list
first so parents can see what to prepare. That order hasn't been tested with
users yet." The supplied choice creates perspective without claiming a measured
outcome. If that reason is unavailable, ask when distinctive voice matters or
write neutrally. Do not invent opinions to raise voice scores or require voice
samples for simple tasks.
