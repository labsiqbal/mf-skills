land-110m.bin is derived from Natural Earth 110m land (ne_110m_land), version 5.1.2,
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/ (data from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector).

Natural Earth is in the public domain. "All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector
map data found on this website are in the public domain. You may use the maps in any
manner, including modifying the content and design, electronic dissemination, and
offset printing." No permission is needed; this notice records the source.

The file is an equirectangular mask of 360 x 180 one-degree cells (column 0 at 180 W,
row 0 at 90 N), one bit per cell, rows of 45 bytes, most significant bit first,
generated by packages/mf-motion/scripts/gen-land-mask.mjs.
